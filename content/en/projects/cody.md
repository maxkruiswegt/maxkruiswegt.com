Cody was a versatile Discord bot crafted in **C#** using **DSharpPlus**. I built it for the Kaizen [Discord server](https://discord.gg/improve), a study community I have helped run since 2022 and the same community that now surrounds the Kaizen app. Its Pomodoro timer and study tracking were, in hindsight, the first prototype of that app, and in 2026 Cody was retired in favor of it.

Kaizen is a community for students and life-long learners aiming to achieve freedom doing work they love. It's a place to collaborate, motivate, and work together to achieve that goal.

Cody delivers an array of valuable features for the server while using a **MySQL** database to efficiently store data:

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Message & Study Logs**: Keep tabs on server activity with message logging. Empower users to track their study time with the `/studytime` command, which provides detailed statistics over a day, week, month, or all time. Use `/studycheck` to check your study time at a specific moment, and compete with others on the study leaderboard using `/studyboard`.
- 📊 **Studygraphs**: Use the `/studygraph` command to visualize your study progress with detailed graphs. Track your study habits over time and identify areas for improvement.
- ⏲️ **Pomodoro Power**: Boost productivity with the `/pomodoro` command, which starts a highly customizable Pomodoro timer, supporting multiplayer sessions for synchronized focus.
- 📋 **Task Management**: Organize your to-dos effortlessly with the `/todo` command.
- 🎓 **Educational Assistance**: Search for universities, define words, and convert time zones with `/university`, `/dictionary`, and `/timestamp`.
- 🚀 **Server Productivity**: Receive motivation, report issues confidentially, and enjoy delightful GIFs of cats and dogs with `/motivation`, `/report`, `/cat`, and `/dog`.
